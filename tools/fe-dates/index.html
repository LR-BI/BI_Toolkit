Academic Year & ILR Dates

Turns a date into an academic year, ILR period and return. Also works out funding age and QAR hybrid end year, and gives the matching T-SQL.

How to use
  1. Date to academic year: pick a date (or click Today) to get the academic year, CALOCC, ILR period and which return collects it.
  2. Funding age: enter a date of birth and pick the academic year to get age on 31 August and the 16 to 18 band. Add a date to get age on that day, e.g. an apprenticeship start.
  3. QAR hybrid end year: enter planned end, actual end and (for FM36 standards) achievement date. It shows the year the aim counts in and why.
  4. Each section has T-SQL underneath that does the same thing. Click Copy and swap in your column names.
  5. The returns table highlights the next ILR deadline. Close dates are only loaded for 2026 to 2027.

    Rules used
    
Academic year: 1 August to 31 July. A date in August 2026 to July 2027 is 26/27.

    
CALOCC: last two digits of the academic year's end year, as returned by [dbo].[bc_InstitutionSettingsCALOCC](). 26/27 is 27.

    
ILR period: August is period 1, July is period 12. R13 and R14 are the year-end returns, after July, with a reference date of 31 July.

    
Age: whole years. A 29 February birthday counts from 1 March in non-leap years.

    
Hybrid end year: the latest of the planned end year, actual end year, achievement year (apprenticeship standards on FM36 only) and reporting year. So a withdrawal counts in the year of its planned end date if that is later than the withdrawal.
